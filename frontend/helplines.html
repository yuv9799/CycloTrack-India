Cyclone Helplines

National emergency numbers for cyclone response in India — clearly labelled and tap-to-call on mobile. In a crisis, call 112 first.

Emergency Contacts

National Emergency Numbers

Keep these numbers handy. On mobile, tap a number to call directly. In a crisis, call 112 first.

Emergency
112

All-India unified emergency number — police, fire and ambulance. Works across most of India on any network.

Call 112
Ambulance
108

National ambulance / medical emergency response service.

Call 108
Fire & Rescue
101

Fire, rescue and related life-and-property emergencies.

Call 101
Disaster Helpline
1078

National Disaster Management helpline for disaster situations (NDMA / State Disaster Management control rooms).

Call 1078
Please verify before you rely on it: Emergency numbers can vary by state and city. Always follow official government advisories (IMD bulletins, NDMA and your State Disaster Management Authority) for your area. This list is a general national reference, not a guarantee of availability in every district.
