How Risk Is Scored

Reading The
Risk Levels

Each region's rating combines three signals from the archive above it.

01
Landfall Frequency

How often a cyclonic or severe cyclonic storm has crossed this stretch of coast over the historical window.

02
Peak Intensity

The highest category reached by storms making landfall here, weighted toward severe and super cyclones.

03
Coastal Exposure

Population density, elevation, and low-lying delta terrain that shape how much damage a landfall causes.
