CycloTrack India is our Smart India Hackathon 2026 submission under the Disaster Management theme — a dashboard that maps, tracks, and archives cyclone risk across the Indian coastline.

Team Nexus SIH 2026 Theme: Disaster Management 4 Core Modules
The Project

Why We're
Building This

India's coastline sees some of the most destructive cyclones on Earth, but risk data, historical records, and live tracking are usually scattered across different agencies and formats.

The Problem

Coastal communities, planners, and researchers often have to piece together cyclone risk from multiple sources — IMD bulletins, historical news archives, and static reports — with no single place to see a region's risk, a storm's history, and live tracking together.

Our Approach

CycloTrack India brings live tracking, a region-by-region risk map, and a 50-year historical archive into one dashboard, with a built-in agent so anyone can ask a plain-language question and get an answer grounded in the data.

Who It's For

Built for the people who need coastal risk information fastest: disaster management authorities, researchers, students, and residents of India's cyclone-prone coastal states.

What's Inside

Four core modules — Home, Cyclone Region, History, and the Agent — cover live monitoring, regional risk scoring, a decade-by-decade archive of major cyclones, and a conversational way to query all of it.
